# Theories Overview

Last updated: 2026-06-08

## Leading Theory

1. [Psoriatic spondyloarthritis with enthesitis](./psoriatic-spondyloarthritis-enthesitis.md)

This is currently the best-fit theory because it ties together longstanding psoriasis, around 15 years of nail pitting/ridges, tendon/attachment-region pain, back/hip/glute stiffness, rib/chest wall symptoms, morning stiffness, and worse symptoms after rest.

## What Matches Best

- Longstanding psoriasis, not recent, for over 10 years.
- Nail pitting/ridges for around 15 years, roughly since 2011.
- Pain around likely enthesis regions: hamstring origins/sit-bone area, medial elbow, hips/glutes/lower back, and rib/chest wall.
- Morning stiffness lasting about 15-60 minutes.
- Worse after sitting or inactivity.
- Chest wall/rib/back pain episodes on 2026-05-26 and 2026-05-27.
- RF, CCP, MCV, Lyme screen, CK, and CRP do not point strongly toward rheumatoid arthritis, Lyme disease, muscle breakdown, or marked systemic inflammation.
- The finalized 2026-06-03 EBV panel suggests recent Epstein-Barr virus infection or recovery/reactivation, which could contribute to a recent systemic symptom layer if the timeline fits.

## What Does Not Match Or Is Missing

- No documented rheumatology exam yet.
- No SI joint/spine imaging yet.
- No confirmed enthesitis, synovitis, dactylitis, sacroiliitis, reduced chest expansion, or reduced spinal mobility.
- HLA-B27 is negative.
- CRP is normal.
- No reported uveitis, inflammatory bowel symptoms, sausage digits, or major fatigue.
- Isotretinoin could be contributing to musculoskeletal symptoms.
- The EBV result does not neatly explain a 6-month enthesis/back/rib pattern by itself, especially without documented fever, sore throat, swollen lymph nodes, or major fatigue.

## Other Active Possibilities

- [Recent Epstein-Barr virus infection or recovery/reactivation](./recent-ebv-infection.md).
- Isotretinoin-related musculoskeletal symptoms.
- Mechanical tendinopathy/overuse with rowing/yoga aggravation.
- Axial spondyloarthritis without clear psoriasis-driven PsA label.
- Connective tissue disease related to positive ANA, currently less supported because the ANA pattern is dense fine speckled and classic symptoms are not prominent.

## Best Next Clarifiers

- Rheumatology exam focused on enthesitis, SI joints, spine/chest expansion, nails, skin, and peripheral joints.
- MRI pelvis/SI joints if axial disease is suspected.
- ESR, repeat CRP, CBC, CMP, urinalysis, urine protein/creatinine, ENA, anti-dsDNA, C3/C4, and possibly anti-DFS70.
- EBV follow-up with the ordering clinician: symptom timing, CBC with differential, liver enzymes, spleen exam/activity precautions if mono is clinically suspected, and whether repeat EBV testing or EBV DNA PCR is useful.
- Track whether symptoms improve, persist, or worsen after stopping isotretinoin.
