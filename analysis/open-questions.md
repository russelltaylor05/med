# Open Questions and Clinician Follow-Up

Use this as the active question list. Move resolved facts into the patient history, timeline, or evidence summary instead of leaving them here indefinitely.

## Symptom pattern

- Is the currently reported 5/10 pain present at rest, with movement, or both, and how much does it limit sleep, walking, head turning, breathing, driving, and work?
- Does pain wake the patient during the second half of the night?
- After 10–30 minutes of gentle movement, does stiffness improve, or does movement predictably worsen pain later that day or the next day?
- Within the broadly affected neck, shoulders, spine, and lower back, is there one focal worst point or a midline-versus-paraspinal pattern? Is it unilateral or bilateral?
- Is the whole-back almost-tingling sensation a skin sensation or a deep muscular “buzzing/contraction” feeling? Is there any true reduced sensation when the skin is touched?
- With lateral/intercostal rib pain into the back, are there cough, coughing blood, rapid heartbeat, unilateral calf swelling, rash, or a sudden one-sided onset? No shortness of breath is currently reported.
- For prescribed cyclobenzaprine 10 mg taken as needed: how many doses have been taken, when was it prescribed, and what other medicines, sedatives, or alcohol are being used?
- Does the tight/pulling posterior-thigh pain extend below the knee or into the calf/foot? Is prolonged sitting a consistent trigger?
- Is ordinary light touch painful, or is the problem mainly deep pressure, massage, and specific loading?
- Are poor sleep, brain fog, sensory sensitivity, or widespread tenderness present?
- Which activities are worst: sitting, driving, squatting, hip hinges, uphill walking, running, rowing, lunges, forward folds, stretching, or spinal movement?
- Are there alternating buttock pain, swollen joints, heel or plantar-fascia pain, current psoriasis/nail activity, bowel inflammation symptoms, or eye redness/pain/light sensitivity?
- Prednisone response: what was the exact first dose/date, daily dose sequence, and prescriber's planned duration/taper? Which sites improved, how quickly, and are morning stiffness, night waking, chest-wall pain, hamstring pain, and elbow pain each absent, reduced, or unchanged at 10 mg? Are there insomnia, mood change, elevated glucose, reflux, fever, or infection symptoms?

## Isotretinoin and exposure timeline

- What were the exact start date, daily dose, dose changes, and cumulative dose for each isotretinoin course?
- Did the musculoskeletal symptoms begin before the latest course, during it, after a dose change, or after a training change?
- Since stopping on 2026-05-01, which symptoms have improved, remained unchanged, or worsened?
- Were preformed-vitamin-A products, cod-liver oil, tetracyclines, fluoroquinolones, statins, steroids, or other relevant drugs used concurrently?
- Was liver or other organ meat eaten frequently during the course?

## Other discriminators

- Was there calorie restriction, fasting, weight loss, a restrictive diet, or unusually high training volume when symptoms began?
- Are there celiac-specific clues such as chronic diarrhea, bloating, greasy stool, unexplained weight loss, mouth ulcers, characteristic rash, or first-degree family history?
- Is the mildly high total bilirubin new or longstanding, and were there any jaundice, dark urine, pale stool, itching, abdominal pain, or unexplained weight loss?
- Were there clear mono-like symptoms or exposures around April–June 2026, and did the longer musculoskeletal course change abruptly during that window?
- Is there a family history of autoimmune disease?
- Do the mild thoracic/lower-cervical degenerative x-ray findings match the pain location and pattern, or are they incidental?

## Follow-up to discuss with clinicians

- Obtain the rheumatology note before repeating the same autoimmune workup without new evidence.
- Discuss a focused sports-medicine/PM&R evaluation and targeted imaging when examination or rehabilitation response does not localize the problem.
- If inflammatory suspicion remains meaningful, ask whether SI-joint MRI or Doppler ultrasound is appropriate despite normal ESR/CRP and negative HLA-B27.
- Ask whether the marked, multisite prednisone response changes the need or timing for rheumatology reassessment and targeted SI-joint/enthesis imaging. The clinical team should know the steroid exposure when interpreting current symptoms and imaging.
- For suspected proximal hamstring tendinopathy, discuss load management and graded strengthening rather than aggressive stretching or rest-only care.
- Review the isotretinoin timeline and full supplement/medication inventory with dermatology or primary care; do not deliberately rechallenge or start high-dose vitamin A.
- Do not take iron empirically: available ferritin, iron saturation, hemoglobin, and MCV did not indicate iron deficiency.
- If celiac clues are present, discuss tissue-transglutaminase IgA plus total IgA before changing gluten intake.
- Seek urgent care for the red-flag symptoms listed in [current-assessment.md](./current-assessment.md#safety).
